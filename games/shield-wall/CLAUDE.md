# Shield Wall — architecture notes

Design notes for `games/shield-wall/`, loaded when Claude Code works in this directory. The root [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) keeps the rules that break things; this file has the reasoning and the model. Past decisions are in [docs/DECISIONS.md](../../docs/DECISIONS.md) (2026-10-15 for this game's design).

The classics cabinet's Space Invaders. A formation of cubes marches down in ranks; one gun under four bunkers shoots back.

- **[engine.js](engine.js)** — pure logic, seeded LCG only (`rand`, the same one Airburst uses).
  - **The seam is Hull Breach's**: `step(w, dt)` reads no input. The shell moves the gun (`nudgeGun`, from the stick or the arrow keys, at up to `GUN_SPEED`) and calls `fire(w)` every frame the fire button or Space is held; `fire` does nothing while a shot is already in the air or the gun is down. So "hold to keep firing" is the shell's idea, and the engine's rule is the classic's: one shot at a time.
  - **One board, portrait** (600×1100). `GUN_Y` is the gun line, `GROUND_Y` the surface under it, and the band below the ground is for a thumb. `FLOOR` (16px above the gun) is where a landing formation ends the run.
  - **The formation** is an 8×5 grid (`COLS`, `ROWS`) addressed by `formation.x/y` (the centre of column 0, row 0) and an `alive` array; `invPos` and `extent` derive everything else. It marches `STEP_X` sideways per step and drops `DROP_Y` and turns at a wall. The step interval (`stepInterval`) shrinks as the formation thins, and with the wave — the genre's famous last-invader sprint. Each wave starts `WAVE_LOWER` lower, up to `LOWER_MAX` waves deep. Invaders that reach the bunkers grind away whatever they overlap (`grindBunkers`).
  - **Bunkers** are 14×10 grids of 5px cells (`bunkerShape` cuts the corners and the doorway). A hit `bite`s a fixed ragged pattern out round the cell struck — upward for a shot, downward for a bomb. **Fixed, not random, on purpose**: how much of a bunker the player shoots away must not change what the invaders do later, so damage costs no rolls (a test pins it).
  - **A shot meets the nearest thing first**: bombs, then invaders, then the saucer — bombs fall from below the formation and the saucer flies above it, so that order is distance along the shot's path.
  - **Shots are swept, not point-tested.** `sweepCells` walks the stretch a shot or bomb covered this step for the first live bunker cell, and `hitInvader`/`hitSaucer`/`hitBomb` test the whole stretch too (`spans`). At 30fps a shot covers 37px and an invader's box is 34px tall, so point tests let shots pass through; the render test caught it, and an engine test pins it at 30fps.
  - **Their fire** comes from the bottom invader of a standing column (`dropBomb`). A share of bombs, `AIMED(wave)` (10% a wave, to half), come from the column nearest the gun instead — without them a player could stand still and shoot, and the bunkers were scenery. Shots and bombs that meet cancel out.
  - **Rolls are on timers, never on events.** The bomb timer takes exactly three rolls per tick (column, next gap, aimed or not) whether or not a bomb can drop; the saucer timer takes three (side, next appearance, value) whether or not one is already crossing. So the number of LCG advances depends only on time, and a seed replays whatever the player does — tested with a played-through run, not a staged one.
  - **Lives**: `START_LIVES` (3), one extra at `EXTRA_LIFE_AT` (1500, once). A bomb on the gun costs a life, clears the air and takes the gun down for `RESPAWN` seconds. A landing ends the run whatever the lives.
  - A wave ends when the formation is gone (`endWave`): `wavesCleared` is the progress value for the unlock chain. `nextWave` rebuilds the formation and the bunkers. `snapshot`/`hydrate` keep everything, seed included; bunkers and the formation are packed as 0/1 strings.
  - **Difficulty curve:** the full-run test's bot is a reaction-time model — it reads where a bomb will cross the gun line, and dodges if it is within `window` px and it notices in time (`react`). It moves the gun at `GUN_SPEED` and fires only when lined up, which is exactly what the v57 controls give a player (v56's one-finger control teleported the gun and fired while moving, so the bot then modelled a different game). At 0.2s/150px it clears ten waves, and holds eight on each of five seeds tried; the test pins five, the wave that will open Bulkhead. A bot that never dodges is down by wave 1-4, which is the test that the bombs matter. Waves take about half a minute with good aim.
- **[shield-wall.html](shield-wall.html)** — the shell. **Two controls in the thumb band under the ground** (v57, the owner's call): a stick in the bottom-left corner, analogue across its width with a small dead zone, and a fire button in the bottom-right that keeps firing while held. Each half of the band belongs to one control and each tracks its own pointer, so both thumbs work at once and lifting one never releases the other; the board above the ground is not a control. v56 had one finger do both (hold anywhere: the gun jumped to the finger and fired), and a simulation of that control showed the player's own shots wearing the bunkers down faster than the bombs did, because it could not move without firing. Both act on press (a gameplay surface; `shared/tap.js` leaves it alone). The controls' geometry (`STICK`, `FIRE`, `CONTROLS_TOP`) lives in the art section, which draws them, and the input code reads it. The march beat plays at most every `MARCH_GAP` (110ms): near the end of a wave the formation steps every 25-45ms and 70ms notes stacked into a buzz. The saucer shows its points as a floater (`gfx.text`), unless the player has switched numbers off. The HUD is written only when a number changes. Banners are raised on false→true *edges* of `waveClear`/`over` in the frame loop. Records **waves held** to `shared/unlocks.js` on each wave clear, saves on a held wave and on page hide with Hull Breach's v45 rule, and a run saved behind the wave banner resumes into the next wave.
- **Art:** invaders are cubes (the absolute rule) in three rank colours, tilting between two march poses; the gun is a sphere under a dome; the stick and fire button are rings in the gun's and the shot's colours, faint at rest and lit under a thumb (UI, so neither cubes nor spheres); bunkers are flat green cells; their bombs are red zigzags and our shot a cyan bolt. Bombs, shot and gun were enlarged after the bench showed them as flecks at phone scale (the board draws at about two thirds size on a phone).
  - **Everything drawn lives between the `art (lifted verbatim …)` and `end art` markers.** `node tools/art-bench.mjs shield-wall` builds **[docs/art/shield-wall-bench.html](../../docs/art/shield-wall-bench.html)** from exactly that text and [tools/shield-wall-bench.template.html](../../tools/shield-wall-bench.template.html): a staged board (thinned formation in both poses, saucer, bombs, shot, bitten bunkers, the gun alive and down) and a live run played by the test suite's bot through the same stick and fire button, so the controls show what it is doing. It needs a page that runs scripts — a browser, the Pages URL, or an artifact — not the Claude app's file preview. Rebuild it after any art change.
  - **Cost:** cubes and the saucer are stamped from a sprite cache, and each bunker is cached as a canvas keyed by a hash of its cells, so a full board with six bombs draws in about 0.13ms in desktop Chromium. The render harness can't make a second canvas, so it exercises the live fallback (`SPRITES_OK`) and measures ~12ms there — that number is the fallback, not the game.
  - **Sounds are its own** (`march`, `laser`, `invaderDown`, `gunDown`, `chip`, `saucer`, `saucerDown` in `shared/audio.js`), checked by `shared/audio.test.js` against the phone-speaker rules from Airburst's round 20.
- **Tests:** [engine.test.js](engine.test.js) (board → the gun → the formation → shooting → invader fire → randomness → waves → saving → full runs), [render-test.mjs](render-test.mjs) (the stick, the fire button, both thumbs at once, the saucer floater, a busy board, the banners) and [resume-test.mjs](resume-test.mjs).
- **Unlock:** the cabinet opens Shield Wall at Airburst wave 5 held (`GATES` in `shared/unlocks.js`), and Shield Wall wave 5 will open Bulkhead.
