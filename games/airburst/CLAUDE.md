# Airburst — architecture notes

Design notes for `games/airburst/`, loaded when Claude Code works in this directory. The root [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) keeps the rules that break things; this file has the reasoning and the model. Past decisions are in [docs/DECISIONS.md](../../docs/DECISIONS.md) (2026-10-14 for this game's design).

The classics cabinet's Missile Command. Warheads fall on six cities; three batteries answer with interceptors that burst where the player taps.

- **[engine.js](engine.js)** — pure logic, seeded LCG only (`rand`, the same one Flak Battery and Feedline use).
  - **The seam is Hull Breach's**: `step(w, dt)` reads no input, and the shell calls `fire(w, x, y)` on a tap, as Hull Breach's calls `launch`. `fire` picks the battery (`baseFor`: nearest standing one with ammunition), so the player aims and the game picks the gun.
  - **One board, portrait** (600×1100). `GROUND_Y` is the surface; `FIRE_FLOOR` (40px above it) is the lowest point an interceptor may be aimed, since a burst on the ground only hits what it protects.
  - Warheads are straight lines from a point above the board to a target on the ground (a city or a battery), positioned by distance travelled `d` along `len`. A warhead touching any burst's *current* radius is destroyed (`blastRadius(t)`: grow, hold, fade). Destroyed warheads do not themselves burst — no chain reactions.
  - **Splitting** starts at `SPLIT_WAVE` (4): a warhead may carry a `splitAt` height and becomes `splitInto(wave)` warheads there, each aimed at its own target. **Every roll a split could need is taken when the warhead is released, for every warhead** — whether it splits, where, and a target for each of up to three children (`kidRolls`) — so the number of LCG advances never depends on what happens later. The first version drew the children's targets at the moment of the split, so shooting a warhead before it split changed every warhead released after it; a hand-staged test passed anyway, and a played-through test (two seeds' worth of releases on a calm wave and a splitting one) is what caught it. `splitSoon(h)` (0..1 over the last `SPLIT_WARN` px) drives the warning, and `fx.split` fires at the split.
  - A battery hit by a warhead loses its remaining ammunition until the next wave; **batteries are rebuilt every wave, cities are not**. A lost city comes back at the end of a wave for every `BONUS_CITY_EVERY` points crossed. Losing the last city ends the run immediately.
  - A wave ends when everything has been released and nothing is left in the air (`endWave`): spare ammunition and standing cities pay, times `scoreMult(wave)`, and `w.lastBonus` holds the breakdown for the banner. `nextWave` advances; the shell sits a banner between waves, which is also where the run is saved.
  - **Difficulty curve:** `waveCount`, `warheadSpeed` and `spawnGap` per wave, all capped. The full-run test's bot, which leads each warhead by the interceptor's real flight time and does not fire twice at one target, holds about 8-9 waves; the test pins 5, the wave that will open the next classic (Shield Wall). A bot that aimed a fixed 0.45s ahead lost on wave 2 — leading properly is the skill the game teaches.
  - `snapshot`/`hydrate` keep everything, including objects in flight and the seed, so a resumed run releases the warheads it would have.
- **[airburst.html](airburst.html)** — the shell. **Touch aims at a crosshair `AIM_LIFT` (64 CSS px) above the fingertip and fires on release**; released off the board is a cancel. The first version fired on press at the point under the finger, which put the finger over the warhead being aimed at. A mouse still fires on press at the cursor. The crosshair's ring is the size of a full burst. Banners are raised on false→true *edges* of `waveClear`/`over` in the frame loop. Records **waves held** (`wavesCleared`) to `shared/unlocks.js` on each wave clear, for the classics chain. Saves on a held wave and on page hide, with Hull Breach's v45 rule (a page where nothing has started leaves a stored run alone). A run saved behind the wave banner resumes into the next wave.
- **Art:** warheads are cubes (invaders, the absolute rule); cities, batteries and bursts are round. Bursts are the accent colour (`#7fd0e6`/`#a8e4f2`), also the shell's `--accent`; a warhead about to split runs hot (`#ffd2a0`) inside a closing ring.
  - **Everything drawn lives between the `art (lifted verbatim …)` and `end art` markers** in the shell, and **[tools/airburst-bench.mjs](../../tools/airburst-bench.mjs)** builds **[docs/art/airburst-bench.html](../../docs/art/airburst-bench.html)** from exactly that text — a staged sky with every element in every state, and a live run at a chosen wave. Judge the art there, in a browser, not from node-canvas. **It needs a page that runs scripts**: a browser opening the file, the Pages URL once merged (`/docs/art/airburst-bench.html`), or its published copy at https://claude.ai/artifact/QF4R1uQzNvNzW84wpfg5Sj (republish after rebuilding). The Claude app's file preview does not run them, and shows a blank board with dead buttons — reported 2026-10-14. The template starts at `<title>`, with no doctype, so the same file publishes as an artifact unchanged. The art section may depend only on `ctx, W, H, L, world, gfx, prefs, reduce, aim, E, TAU` and the glow primitives, or the bench stops building. Rebuild it after any art change.
  - **Cost, measured on a 40-warhead late-wave sky in node-canvas:** 21.5ms a frame as first shipped. Cubes are now stamped from a sprite cache (32 rotation steps; 7.4ms → 0.09ms for forty), trails are the last `TRAIL_LEN` (240px) rather than the whole line back to the top, interceptor streaks `INT_TRAIL` (150px) rather than the whole line from the battery (six full lines cost 3.7ms), and burst discs are one additive fill. Real late waves peak around 30 warheads at once with nobody firing. The sprite path needs a second canvas, so the render harness exercises the live fallback (`SPRITES_OK`, Choke Point's pattern).
  - **Sounds are its own** (`launch`, `burst`, `intercept`, `split`, `impact`, `dry` in `shared/audio.js`), re-voiced in v55 after a phone heard none but the impact. `shared/audio.test.js` pins two rules for them: no sine or triangle below 300Hz (no harmonics to carry a low note through a phone speaker), and nothing quieter than Flak Battery's fire sound.
- **Tests:** [engine.test.js](engine.test.js) (board → determinism → firing → warheads → splitting → waves → saving → full run), [render-test.mjs](render-test.mjs) (including the crosshair: touch aims above and fires on release, slide-off cancels, mouse fires on press) and [resume-test.mjs](resume-test.mjs).
- **Unlock:** the cabinet opens Airburst at Hull Breach level 3 cleared (`GATES` in `shared/unlocks.js`). Hull Breach records levels cleared, and seeds that record from its level-select history so a pre-v53 player is not made to repeat them.
