Guns

Every emplacement carries one gun type. Damage, reload and round speed below are multipliers on the base line, so 2.4 damage means a round hits 2.4× as hard as the starting cannon's, and a reload of 1.9 means it fires at a bit over half the rate. Upgrades bought with scrap multiply through these, which is why the same branch is worth different amounts on different guns.
