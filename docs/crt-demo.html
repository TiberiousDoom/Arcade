Vector / CRT look — four techniques

All Canvas 2D, no libraries, no WebGL. Panels 3 and 4 are the two different answers to "can it look 3D".

1 · Current style

Flat fills with a gradient and a drop shadow. Reads as painted plastic — solid, but inert.

2 · Emissive vector

Same shapes as line art: each stroke drawn four times, wide+dim to narrow+white, composited additively. Now it emits.

3 · Volume: dark fill + glowing rim + extrusion

The middle path. A dark body, an emissive edge, and an offset side face. Objects read as solid and dimensional without being shaded models.

4 · True wireframe 3D

Real perspective projection — rotating solids drawn as edges. Authentic Battlezone. Pure arithmetic, no renderer.
