Gun Bench

The five gun portraits, drawn by the repo's own code — shared/glow.js and drawCannonPortrait, lifted verbatim. Change the elevation here before it changes in the game. The railgun keeps its own offset from the slider — it is a flat-trajectory gun.

True brightness: dark fills under additive glow, on the board's own ground.

Drawn on a canvas, then ported

The v49 redraw was designed on a design canvas first, variants side by side, and ported coordinate for coordinate. Change the art here or there first, never by nudging numbers in the shell.

Four mounts, read first

Towed outriggers for the cannon and mortar · bunker casemate for the autocannon · deck plate and drum for the railgun · mound for the ion: a ball behind a low lip. What a gun stands on is the widest thing on the card, so it is read before the barrel.

Every gun pivots on a sphere

Invaders are cubes and defenders are spheres. Each gun's trunnion is a small lit ball, and the ion's mount is one big one. The highlight is counter-rotated, so it stays upper-left at any elevation.

The breech sits behind the trunnion

A gun does not pivot at the back of its own barrel. The breech swings down as the muzzle comes up, and the equilibrator ties the cradle to the carriage in a visible triangle. The clearest cue that the gun is elevating rather than merely pointing.

Six bores, four tubes

Only the front of a rotary cluster shows side-on, so four tubes are drawn, the far two dimmer. All six bores show on the muzzle face, where they can actually be counted. A belt of rounds feeds the drum.

No tube around the rails

The railgun is two rails held by spacer rings, with the charge line and the round between them, and a finned power unit behind. A second barrel is a third rail above, sharing the upper one.

The mortar lobs without saying so

Same carriage as the cannon, a short fat tube nearly vertical, and a screw jack holding it up. A drawn trajectory was tried on the canvas and read as a shot, not as a gun.

The ion is not a tube

An insulator stack of alternating discs out of the ball, ending in a second, smaller sphere that sparks. Purple since v49. It works on a different principle from the rest, and should look like it.

The second barrel goes on top

The engine's offset is positive, but below the main tube a second one runs through the cannon's recoil cylinder and the mortar's screw jack. A side view has no side a viewer can check, so it takes the clear one. The board draws the real offsets.

Why 60° and not 70°

Past about 65° the equilibrator, cradle and breech fold in behind the tube and the gun loses the triangle of linkage that reads as artillery. Drag the slider past 70 and watch it happen.

Silhouette exposure lifts the gamma hard — deliberately unfaithful, and useful because shape is the only thing it shows. It is how this art gets checked at all: a headless canvas renders the additive glow far dimmer than a phone does, so a faithful capture is unreadable and an is there ink here test answers yes wherever it is pointed.
