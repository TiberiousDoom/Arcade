What to look at

Can a warhead be told from its trail at phone size? Does the split warning read before the split? Does a burst say how big it is? Is the crosshair visible without covering what it aims at?

Silhouette lifts the gamma hard — not what the game looks like, but what its shapes are. Drawing code here is byte-identical to the game's; the lift is applied after the draw, never inside it.
